Journal Ln

Notes for travelers and people thinking about moving to Dallas.

Moving to Dallas: what actually changes Relocation

The first thing you notice is space. Bigger kitchens, real closets, a driveway. The second is that almost everything is a drive away, so plan your search around your commute, not just the house.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are higher than many newcomers expect, so budget for them when you compare homes. Summers are long and hot; a good AC system matters more than a fireplace.

And people really do say hello to strangers here. You get used to it.

A weekend around Fair Park Travel

Fair Park is home to the State Fair of Texas every fall and to a collection of Art Deco buildings worth a slow walk any time of year. Check the calendar before you visit; something is usually happening on the grounds.

Deep Ellum is a short ride away for live music, murals and late-night food. Downtown and the Arts District are close too, so a stay near Fair Park puts you in the middle of it all.

What I learned in my first year hosting Hosting

List on more than one platform, and keep every calendar in sync so you never double-book. Price your weeknights to stay competitive; weekends mostly take care of themselves.

Write clear house rules, say yes to guests who are upfront about their plans, and walk event guests through the space at check-in. Small habits like that earn the reviews.
